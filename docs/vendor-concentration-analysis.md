# Executive Question: How difficult is it to enter the unmanned systems market?

## Analysis Method:
Grouped the drone filtered data set of 171 contracts, FY22-25 by the recipient_parent_name, cleaned name inconsistencies that included punctuation errors.Calculated each vendors market share, then HHI sum of squared shares * 10,000. Also calculated the top 5 concentration ratio and recalculated HHI with General Atomics excluded to see competitiveness of market under the dominant player.

## Headline Finding:
Overall HHI: 3,296, highly concentrated
Top 5 vendors capture 90.2% of total contract dollars
General Atomics alone brings in 2.9 billion dwarfing every other vendor
HHI excluding General Atomics 1,878, moderately concentrated
Other big competitors AeroVironment $690 + 74 Million, Northrop Grumman $605 Million, Anduril $341 Million, SRC $256 Million, RTX $132 Million, L3Harris $ 95 Million

## Why it matters for Ironclad Dynamics:
Market Beneath General Atomics is only moderately concentrated not locked up, several big vendors can be realistically competed with for market share such as L3Harris and RTX
More numerous, smaller contracts are being issued, which matches a market where mid tier vendors can be active

General atomics dominance means largest opportunities are out of reach
High overall HHI could be due to barriers to entry that exist outside of General Atomics
