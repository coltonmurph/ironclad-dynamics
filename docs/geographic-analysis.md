# Executive Question: Where should we focus our business, Ironclad Dynamics, geographically?

## Analysis Method:
Grouped df_drones by primary_place_of_performance_state_name, and calculated spend and contract count per state.Pulled California's vendor breakdown. Checked whether Navy's lower vendor concentration also held geographically filtered df_drones by navy contracts only group by state.Drilled further in county and vendor

## Headline Findings:
California dominated $4.61B, 88 contracts, New York Second closest $265.2M, 21 contracts
California home to key vendors like General Atomics, AeroVironment, Northrop Grumman and Anduril making up for 98% of states total spend
Navy Specific breakdown - Maryland Leads with $98.1M, spend spread across 8 states total
Two specific vendors, that cover two counties in Maryland

## Why it Matters
Recommendation: Prioritize the Maryland Defense testing corridor Aberdeen/Harford County and Patuxent River/Saint Mary's County as Ironclads geographic focus for early Navy business development, rather than attempting to establish a california presence.
Pros- 
Directly reinforces Navy as entry point recommendation
Real existence proof:SURVICE and Sabre are already winning this exact kind of work at Ironclad's likely scale, proven working opportunity. 
Avoids likely futile competition with the four dominating players in the California market

Cons-
Based on only two vendors and two counties
Says nothing about facility relocation cost
Assumes Maryland's pattern holds going forward

## Limitations

Place of performance reflects where contracted work is executed not headquarters - some vendors could be non local
Small underlying sample outside of california makes states sensitive to any new contract
same total_obligated_amount limitation noted throughout project - multi year contract values are attributed to start year
Fourth and Final piece of analysis

Use navy as entry point, while also teaming with dominating players in the market, focus on maryland for near term relationship building
