# Executive Question: Who should Ironclad sell to?

## Analysis Method: 
grouped df_drones by awarding_sub_agency_name, computed total spend and contract count per agency and then check General Atomics spend specifically by agency. This was done to check if there dominance was agency specific. This intial check suggested Army might be more open due to the absence of General Atomics so I Calculated HHI per agency(rather than market-wide) to test that hypothesis, which revelaed Army was still highly concentrated, just under a different vendor, AeroViroment.

## Headline Finding: 
Air Force $3.74B, HHI 6,191 - dominated by General Atomics 76.9% of air force spend
Army $1.07B, HHI 5,385 - dominated by AeroVironment 70.4% of army spend
SOCOM $357M, HHI 9,144 - most concentrated of all
Navy $190M, HHI 1,939 - only moderately concentrated segment, 13 distinct vendors, no single dominant player.

## Why it Matters for Ironclad Dynamics:
Recommendation 1 -
Use the Navy as near term entry point

Pros: least concentrated market, diverse vendor base, contract sizes plausible for a mid size firm to win directly

Cons: smallest total market by far(47M/year), only an entry point should not be whole strategy

Recommendation 2:
Team with the dominating players rather than against them

Pros: turns the multi billion dollar contracts that are held by the dominating players into accessible money through subcontracting

Cons: dependent on actually landing subcontracts which isnt guaranteed and data cannot confirm

Recommendation 3:

Deprioritize SOCOM:

 Highest concentration along with smallest near term market and typically highest security vetting

## Limitations

Navy HHI is based only on 20 contracts across 4 years, so the small samples makes this sensitive to any new contract.
Teaming partner recommendation is just a strategic move data cant prove it.
Assumes Ironclads project are credible and can work adjacnet to General Atomics or AeroViroment
Same total_obligated_amount limitation noted in the growth and vendor concentration docs
